Potion Panic · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Publisher: NoffTech LLC. Last updated 17 September 2026.

This policy covers Potion Panic (Potion Panic: Chaos Color Sort) on iPhone and iPad.

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Game progress on this device

Campaign progress, settings, energy, Chaos Run state, and similar play data are stored on your device so the game works offline.

iCloud Key-Value Store

When you are signed into iCloud, we may sync a small wallet snapshot (coins, gems, tickets, owned cosmetics / pass skins) through Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Store so those purchases and balances can follow you to another device on the same Apple ID. This is not CloudKit and is not a full game save in iCloud. Apple operates iCloud under Apple’s privacy policy.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center alias and player ID for achievements, leaderboards, challenges, and activities. Signing in is optional; levels and boosters stay available if you skip.

Purchases

StoreKit processes coin packs, gem packs, the Starter Pack, and the Chaos Pass subscription. Apple handles payment. We keep the entitlements needed to grant what you bought and to show subscription status. Manage or cancel Chaos Pass in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Analytics

We rely on Apple’s App Store analytics where available. We do not ship third-party ad or attribution SDKs in Potion Panic.

Children

Potion Panic is rated for a general audience with optional Game Center contests. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising.
